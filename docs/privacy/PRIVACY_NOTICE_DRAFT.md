# Synora privacy notice — DRAFT (not published, not legal advice)

Status: DRAFT for founder review. Provider facts below were read from each
provider's own pages on 2026-11-08; anything not confirmed there is marked
NOT VERIFIED. Re-check before publishing — terms change.

## What this notice covers

1. **The public portfolio assistant** (`POST /api/public/assistant`, used on
   keshav-kumar-singh.vercel.app). Anyone can type a question. No account.
2. **Synora accounts** (registration, login). Closed beta: `REGISTRATION_MODE=invite`.

## What we send to AI providers

| Data | Sent to a model provider? |
|---|---|
| A visitor's question to the public assistant (max 500 chars) + published portfolio text | Yes — to one provider in the fallback chain |
| Account email, password, profile, billing | **No.** Never sent to any model provider. |
| Signed-in users' conversations and documents | **Not through the free-tier chain.** That chain is wired only to the public assistant. |

Rule for the product: **user data must not go to a provider that trains on
prompts.** The public assistant is the only caller of the free-tier chain, and
visitors are told not to type personal data (see "Notice shown to visitors").

### What the code does about it

- Mistral is skipped unless `FREE_TIER_TRAINING_OPTOUT_CONFIRMED` contains
  `mistral` (the founder sets this only after opting out in Mistral's Admin
  Console). Default: skipped.
- Google AI Studio free tier is not in the chain and must not be added for any
  path that carries user data.
- SambaNova and OpenRouter `:free` models are in the chain but flagged above as
  NOT VERIFIED for training. The public assistant only carries a visitor's
  short question and public portfolio text.

## Notice shown to visitors (proposed text)

> Answers are AI-generated by Synora from the published portfolio. Your question
> is sent to a third-party AI provider to produce the answer. Please do not
> type personal or confidential information. We do not store your question.

"We do not store your question" is true only if request bodies are not logged;
the route logs error class names only and the 2 KB body is not persisted. This
is LOCALLY VERIFIED by reading the route, not PRODUCTION VERIFIED.

## Accounts

- Registration is invite-only by default (`REGISTRATION_MODE=invite`).
- Email verification tokens are issued at registration; delivery needs a real
  mail transport (see `SYNORA_OPERATIONS.md`).
- Data controller contact, retention periods, deletion and access requests,
  and the legal basis are **not decided** — founder/legal decision.
